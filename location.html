Places to Visit in Cambodia

Cambodia is a country full of diverse places to explore. From ancient temples to beautiful beaches, there is something for everyone. Here are some of the top places to visit in Cambodia:

Top Places to Visit in Cambodia

  • Siem Reap

    • - Mainly known for its ancient temples and peaceful vibe.

    • Angkor Wat
    • Bayon Temple
    • Ta Prom
    • Kulen Mountain



  • Phnom Penh

    • - The capital city of Cambodia, known for its rich history and vibrant culture.

    • Royal Palace
    • Wat Phnom
    • Tuol Sleng Genocide Museum




  • Koh Rong Island

    • - A beautiful island known for its pristine beaches and clear waters.





  • Kampot

    • - A charming town known for its French colonial architecture and natural beauty.

    • La Plantation
    • Kep National Park
