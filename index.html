About Cambodia

Cambodia is a country located in Southeast Asia, bordered by Thailand, Laos, and Vietnam. It is known for its rich history, culture, and stunning landscapes. The country is home to the famous Angkor Wat temple complex, which is one of the largest religious monuments in the world.


Fun Fact: Cambodia is the only country in the world that has a flag with a building on it. The building is Angkor Wat, which is a temple complex in Cambodia and one of the largest religious monuments in the world.



Cambodia has a diverse culture influenced by its history and neighboring countries. The official language is Khmer, and the majority of the population practices Theravada Buddhism. The country has a tropical climate with distinct wet and dry seasons.

Tourism is a significant part of Cambodia's economy, with visitors drawn to its ancient temples, beautiful beaches, and vibrant cities. Popular destinations include Siem Reap, Phnom Penh, and Sihanoukville.
