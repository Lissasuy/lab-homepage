Places to Visit in Cambodia

Cambodia is a country full of diverse places to explore. From ancient temples to beautiful beaches, there is something for everyone. Here are some of the top places to visit in Cambodia:

  • Siem Reap - Mainly known for its ancient temples.
    • Angkor Wat
    • Bayon Temple
    • Ta Prohm
    • Kulen Mountain
  • Phnom Penh - The capital city of Cambodia.
    • Royal Palace
    • Wat Phnom
    • Tuol Sleng Genocide Museum
  • Koh Rong Island
  • Kampot
    • La Plantation
    • Kep National Park
