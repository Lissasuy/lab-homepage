A Short Introduction to Cambodia

Cambodia is a country that is located in Southeast Asia, and it borders Thailand, Laos, and Vietnam. It is greatly known for its rich history and culture. It houses Angkor Wat, one of the world's largest religious monuments. If you want to learn more about Cambodia through first-hand exxperience, please visit this website.

Cambodia is the only country in the world that has a flag with a building on it. The building is Angkor Wat, which is a temple complex in Cambodia and one of the largest religious monuments in the world.
